2. CORE APPLICATION URLS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
/login             -> Main landing & authentication page (Jenny Scott theme)
/dashboard         -> Executive KPI metrics & monthly revenue area chart
/products          -> Catalog of items, add-ons, and variant attributes
/plans             -> Multi-interval pricing tiers (monthly, yearly, weekly)
/subscriptions     -> Subscription lifecycle registry with search & filters
/invoices          -> Tax invoices, outstanding balance, and due dates
/payments          -> Multi-channel collections (UPI, Cards, Bank Transfer)
/discounts         -> Percentage & flat promo campaigns with redemption limits
/billing-run       -> 1-click execution of the automated invoicing engine
/portal            -> Client self-service portal (subscriptions & receipts)
/portal/invoice/:id-> Clean printable tax invoice receipt

3. POSTGRESQL DATABASE OBJECTS (SUPABASE)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
TABLES:
  - customers            : Client accounts (name, email, company)
  - profiles             : Maps auth.users to roles ('admin' | 'customer')
  - products             : Base catalog software items & services
  - product_variants     : Variant options & extra pricing
  - taxes                : GST and sales tax rates
  - plans                : Pricing tiers linked to products & cadences
  - discounts            : Promotion rules, minimum criteria & usage caps
  - subscriptions        : Customer contracts & next_billing_dates
  - subscription_lines   : Items & quantities per subscription
  - invoices             : Generated billing period tax documents
  - invoice_lines        : Breakdown lines with applied tax rates
  - payments             : Recorded settlements against invoices

DATABASE VIEWS:
  - dashboard_summary    : Aggregated MRR, active subs, overdue counts
  - monthly_revenue      : Historical monthly revenue for chart visualization
  - invoice_balances     : Invoices joined with payments & days overdue
  - overdue_invoices     : Invoices where due_date < current_date
  - upcoming_renewals    : Subscriptions renewing within the next 7 days
  - billing_queue        : Subscriptions due for invoicing today or earlier

DATABASE TRIGGERS:
  - trg_set_next_billing : Auto-assigns next_billing_date on activation
  - trg_check_payment    : Prevents settlements exceeding invoice balance
  - trg_mark_paid        : Flips invoice status to 'Paid' upon full balance

STORED PROCEDURES:
  - generate_due_invoices() : Batch engine that processes billing_queue,
                              calculates discounts & taxes, creates invoices,
                              and updates subscription renewal dates.
